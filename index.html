About This Visualization

Why weeks?

Thinking in years makes life feel infinitely long. Breaking it down into weeks shifts your perspective. A week is small enough to plan, yet large enough to accomplish a meaningful milestone.

Why 20-40 are the most productive years?

The highly creative, energetic, and risk-taking years of career execution typically fall between ages 20 and 40. This 20-year window represents your peak capital-building phase.

Compounding

Consistent weekly effort compounds dramatically. Improving just 1% each week creates a 67% increase in ability over a single year. Don't underestimate small, regular actions.
